Welcome to Browser Lock

Protect your browsing session with a master password.

Set up 3 security questions to recover your password if forgotten. Answers must be at least 3 characters.

For your protection: if you ever forget both your master password and your security answers, you can request a full reset. So that nobody else can do this easily, there is a waiting period that you choose now. Until it ends, the browser stays locked.

The waiting period protects you: if someone else tries to reset your browser, you have time to notice and cancel it with your master password. During the waiting period the browser stays locked.
